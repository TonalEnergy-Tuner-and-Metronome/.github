## Chromatic Tuning and Intonation

The chromatic tuner analyzes incoming sound and indicates how closely the detected pitch matches the intended note. Visual feedback helps musicians identify notes that are sharp or flat and make adjustments while playing.

Typical uses include:

- Checking instrument tuning before rehearsal.
- Monitoring intonation during sustained notes.
- Comparing performance against a reference pitch.
- Adjusting tuner sensitivity for different instruments.
- Reviewing pitch consistency during practice.
- Exploring supported transposition and temperament settings.

Results depend on microphone quality, ambient noise, the selected instrument range, and the stability of the incoming signal.
